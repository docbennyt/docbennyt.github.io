My story

How I got into tech.

2020.
WHERE IT STARTED

I started coding during the Covid-19 lockdown after watching Game Shakers and thinking, “I could build something like that.” A mentor introduced me to C and C++. Over the years I taught myself web development and backend engineering, then got interested in product design and automation.

At Afriform Software, I worked as an Associate Developer from November 2025 to July 2026, contributing to TimelessHR, AutoSales and the company website. I worked on features that let staff update adverts, programmes and blogs themselves, and helped improve search indexing.

I am now building aiDo. GuestAI helps hospitality teams handle guest enquiries and reservations. CalenderZW brings university timetables into the calendar students already use. I am also working on aiDo DMs for businesses that take orders and bookings through messages.

I am studying BTech Computer Science at Harare Institute of Technology while working on these products. I also run Scholarz Nation to help students find opportunities and useful learning resources.
