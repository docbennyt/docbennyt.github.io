Autosales — WhatsApp AI SaaS

Role Full Stack Developer (sole developer)
Year 2024 – Present
Live autosales.afriformsoftware.com ↗
Next.js Node.js PostgreSQL Redis n8n WhatsApp Business API Tailwind CSS AI Agents

The Problem

Zimbabwean and African SMEs receive hundreds of WhatsApp messages daily with no system to handle, qualify, or convert them into sales. Human agents are slow, inconsistent, and expensive.

What Was Built

A multi-tenant WhatsApp AI SaaS platform where businesses deploy AI sales agents on their WhatsApp numbers. Each tenant gets isolated data, their own agent configuration, and a CRM pipeline view.

Key Features

  • Multi-tenant architecture (isolated per business)
  • AI-powered WhatsApp sales conversations
  • n8n workflow automation backend
  • CRM pipeline with lead stages
  • Credit-based billing system
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) API support
  • Human takeover mode (agent → human handoff)
  • Conversation analytics dashboard

Outcome

Live and serving real business clients. My first product demonstrating the commercial viability of AI-powered sales automation for the African SME market.
