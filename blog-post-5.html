Building from Zimbabwe: The Hunger Advantage

When you start coding during a lockdown with limited tech resources, you learn resourcefulness fast. Here is why builders from Africa are bringing unmatched grit and innovation to the global tech scene.

Doing More with Less

African developers don’t have the luxury of taking top-tier hardware, unlimited high-speed internet, or constant electricity for granted. Every line of code, every framework installed, and every API deployed is heavily optimized. This constraint breeds a specific kind of systems-level thinking. We know how to squeeze every drop of performance out of a server because we had to.

The Hunger to Succeed

Talent is equally distributed; opportunity is not. When the opportunity finally presents itself, African devs grab it with both hands. It's a hunger that can't be taught in a bootcamp. We aren't just coding for fun; we are coding to change the trajectory of our communities.
